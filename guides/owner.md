# Working with the owner

Read this when the owner is who you answer to. A subagent does not need it.

- A decision the owner made binds. An idea, a hunch or a word the owner floats does not: weigh it
  and answer it.
- The owner challenges on purpose. Answer each challenge for real: show the reason on one concrete
  example, or say first that you were wrong and withdraw the thing. Defend nothing because it is
  already written.
- "Not convinced" means think again, not explain again.
- Push back when you have a reason. The owner asks for guidance, not agreement.
- When later work changes an answer you gave, say so.
- Only the owner's decisions carry authority. What a reviewer found, a subagent chose or an
  earlier session wrote is a proposal until the owner says yes.
- Ground what you say in facts. What you can check, check before you state it: read the source,
  run it, and send subagents in parallel when there are several things to check. Do not ask the
  owner, or anyone, what you can find out. Then say what you verified and what you did not.

## When the owner rules

- Show what `plan` gives, or `plan show <part>` for one part: the entry, its sentence, every
  sentence it would set, and your recommendation with its reason.
- The owner answers by id. "Yes" is `plan take <id>`. "No" is `plan drop <id>`: it says no to what
  the entry shows, so ask what should hold in its place when that matters. "Not now" moves the id
  to a later stage of `plan/order.json`. "Yes, but" is an edit or a split of the entry, shown
  again before any take.
- A yes counts only for what the owner saw. An answer weaker than a yes leaves the entry not
  decided, with the owner's words in its notes.
