A few things to know.

Do I need to change my editor?+

No. Choose your preferred editor and terminal in Silo. Open a project from Files, or use SSH access with a compatible editor or coding agent.

How do remote computers work?+

Install Silo on both devices. Enable SSH access and Allow connections from other devices on the owning device, then connect from your device. Keep Silo running on the owning device. Manage its GitHub account, secrets, exports, and imports there.

Remote setup guide →
What happens when I stop a computer?+

Your files survive stops, restarts, and app updates. Quitting Silo stops its local computers and preserves their files; computers on other devices keep running.

Do I need to connect GitHub?+

No. GitHub is optional. You can create a computer and work in its terminal without connecting an account.
