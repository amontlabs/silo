# What the owner decides, and what agents decide

Put forward after the owner said on 18 November 2026 that agreeing with engine statements the
owner cannot assess brings nothing.

The rest of the line: the owner says where an explanation lost them and what they have seen go
wrong in practice, which becomes cases for the simulator's fakes. Mechanisms are checked against
the promises by the simulator, by reviews and by engineers the owner trusts.

## Widened on 22 November 2026

The owner, asked whether agents should take the entries about engineering practice themselves:
"it doesn't really make sense to me that I'd need to go through all the decisions about generic
engineering practices if you're confident they are good and applicable, but rather spend my time on
what's specific to silo". On the bar an agent proposed, which included contradicting nothing the
owner had decided: "maybe if really I decided something strong, but I mean if earlier I accepted
something without much thought and there is a new, better practice, it shouldn't be refused just
for the sake of it". And: "I want you to do all of that, and for me to have remaining only
opinionated decisions".

The owner's reason: the practices come from engineers the owner trusts (TigerStyle, Guillotine,
Zig), and the owner takes part when the work starts, so anything not understood or not liked shows
then.

Order of the work, as the owner set it: first every decision, taken ones included, is read for
contradictions and cleaned up; then the plan tool records an entry taken by agents, and the
Workshop shows it as decided by agents; then the entries that meet the bar are taken, and only
the opinionated ones go to the owner.
