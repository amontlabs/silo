## Proposed

Why: A question that widens nothing has nowhere to wait, so an agent in a fork stops or guesses.

- Rule: A question sits in the one queue of what waits on you, beside approvals.
- A question with fixed options is answered with buttons.
- A blocking question can have a time limit, after which the agent goes on with its recommendation.

## Open

- While a question is open, may the agent try each option in its own fork, and drop the others once you answer?
  - Suggested: Later, as a choice the agent makes, since every option costs a fork.
- Can you also leave work items there for your agent to take on?
  - Suggested: No: agent-lives-inside hands work to your agent in its own conversation.
