## Proposed

Why: The agent doing the work is a poor judge of it, and nobody watches every fork.

- Rule: A review fork has no secrets and read-only GitHub, and it is never offered for merge.
- A reviewer reports on three levels only: hacky, broken after being declared complete, and stuck.
- Where possible, the reviewer runs on a different provider from the work.
