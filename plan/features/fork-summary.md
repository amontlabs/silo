## Proposed

Why: A fork's name and a long diff do not say what happened in it.

- The account comes in two-minute, five-minute and fifteen-minute readings.
- The page opens in the browser and can be published where the computer's rules allow, for example one repository's GitHub Pages.
- Rule: The account is labelled as written by a model and kept apart from the engine's own records.

## Open

- Which model writes it: one on your subscription, a small local model, or none, with a version built from the records?
  - Suggested: A small local model, with the records-only version when it is missing, so no subscription is spent.
- Does the account also come as slides, video or audio for a reviewer?
  - Suggested: No; a page is enough for the first user.
