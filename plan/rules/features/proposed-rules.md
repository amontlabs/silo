# Rules proposed from the record

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Main ran with its network open for two days. Silo proposes to
limit it to the 14 hosts it actually reached. You remove one you do not know and accept the rest.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). You get a limited network without writing the list. It costs the two open
  days, and care when you accept: a host that something hostile reached in those days is on the list
  too.
- Leave it out. You read the record and write each rule yourself. It costs enough effort that most
  computers would stay open.

Recommended: build it. A proposal changes nothing until you accept it, so the only risk is accepting
without reading, and the list is short enough to read.
