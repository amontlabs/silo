# Rules and precautions

What Silo does by itself: a rule on a fact, a precaution on a report or an opinion.

## For

To let Silo act on what it knows while you are away, without letting something that can be
wrong grant anything or destroy anything.

## Why it is so

- **Two ways to act, told apart by their trigger** (`rule`, `precaution`, `less-sure-less-power`,
  `law-of-triggers`, `grant-needs-a-fact-or-you`).
- **Kinds of action** (`kind-before-design`, `kinds-of-action`). Still to decide: `why-kinds-first`,
  `word-grant`, `word-revoke`.
- **A precaution is the only way an opinion acts** (`opinion-acts-through-a-precaution`,
  `what-a-precaution-may-do`, `where-precautions-live`).
- **When several rules apply** (`which-rule-wins`, `rule-order-never-matters`).

## One example

A rule allows crates.io, and another alerts on any new host. A program in the computer connects
to paste.rs. No rule allows it, so it is denied, and you are alerted.
