# What a report or an opinion may lead Silo to do

The principle `less-sure-less-power` is the law these sentences apply.

## Who or what triggers something decides what it may do

| Triggered by | Through | May do |
|---|---|---|
| You, now | a click or a command | anything |
| A fact | a rule you set | anything the rule says |
| A report or an opinion | a precaution you set | only protective actions |

## The two ways Silo acts

| Word | Triggered by | May do |
|---|---|---|
| Rule | A fact | Anything you wrote in it. A rule is exact. |
| Precaution | A report or an opinion | Only protective actions. A precaution can be wrong. |

## Checks

`rule-reads-only-facts` is checked by a simulator whose inside lies: a fact is what the engine or
the boundary saw.
