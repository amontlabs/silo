# An opinion acts through a precaution

`how-an-opinion-acts` said: "A classifier never acts. Its opinion leads to an action only through
a precaution you set."

Its second sentence stays true. Its first no longer does: an extension may deny, hold or pause by
itself, and granting it that tool is what sets the precaution (`extension-may-protect` in the part
`extensions`).

Put forward by an agent on 21 November 2026, so that no taken decision contradicts another. The
owner has not seen this wording.
