# Which rule wins

The record also says: "Everything is denied unless a rule allows it or you say yes to an ask."
The part on the yes to an ask is settled by `kinds-of-action`: your yes is a grant. The part
in force is "no rule means deny", in `deny-beats-hold-beats-allow`.

`rule-order-never-matters` is the record's sentence without its first word, "So", which tied it
to the sentence before.
