# A routine stretch

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Between 13:00 and 13:40, claude #2 ran 212 commands in main. Most
were builds and tests, and three times it ran the same failing install.

The options:

- Collapse what is routine (recommended). The 40 minutes show as a few lines: what you asked at
  13:00, then "13:02 to 13:31, 180 commands, 12 failed", with Silo's opinion that it went round in
  circles on an install, then the checkpoint at 13:35. It costs one click to see the 180 commands.
- Show every line. All 212 commands, one under the other, with a filter. It costs scrolling through
  212 lines to find the three installs that matter.

Recommended: collapse. You open the Timeline to find a moment, and a few lines you can read are
faster than 212 you have to search.
