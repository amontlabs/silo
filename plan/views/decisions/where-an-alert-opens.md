# Where an alert leads

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. At 14:31 a rule alerts you that codex in main sent 2.1 MB to
paste.rs.

The options:

- The Timeline, at that moment (recommended). You see the connection, the command before it that
  uploaded a file, and the reply in which codex decided to run it, and you can fork from just
  before. It costs a step for an alert about numbers, such as a full disk, where the Disk place says
  more; the alert leads there too.
- The place of its kind. A host alert opens Network on paste.rs, a disk alert opens Disk. It costs
  what the agent was doing and why: you see the host and the 2.1 MB, not what led to them.

Recommended: the Timeline. After an alert you want to know what led to it, and only the Timeline
shows the moment with what came before.
