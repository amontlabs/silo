# Asks and grants in the record

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. On Monday at 15:10 you said yes to codex in main reaching
api.stripe.com with the secret stripe-test. On Tuesday an ask from main ran out while you were away.
A week later you want to know who let main reach Stripe, and whether it asked you for anything else.

The options:

- Every answer, a no and an ask nobody answered included (recommended). The record shows Monday's
  yes, what passed and what Stripe answered, and Tuesday's ask that ended unanswered, with why. It
  costs a few lines in the record for each ask, which you would not notice.
- Only what you granted. The record keeps your yeses and what they let through. It costs telling "I
  said no" from "it never asked me", and asks that ran out leave no trace.

Recommended: every answer. A no and an ask nobody answered are part of what happened as much as a
yes, and the record is where you look for what happened.
