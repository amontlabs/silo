# Disk

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Your Mac has 20 GB free. In main, six copies of target/ take 78
GB and nine old worktrees take 31 GB, and two commits in one of them were never pushed.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). You purge the 78 GB that a rebuild brings back, and you see the two
  unpushed commits before you drop the worktrees. It costs opinions on who wrote what and on what
  comes back, shown as opinions, and some lines that say "unattributed".
- Leave it out. You use a disk analyser inside main. It costs the link to agents and runs, and the
  answer to what a delete would lose.

Recommended: build it. Agents fill a disk fast, and this is the place where you free space knowing
what comes back.
