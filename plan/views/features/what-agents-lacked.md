# What agents lacked

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Over a week, agents in main write "docker is not installed",
"could not reach the staging database" and "the tests take 9 minutes, I ran only some".

The options:

- Build it. Agents write what they lacked through Silo, and the app lists it for each computer,
  marked as the agents' own words. It costs a new kind of line in the record, written by agents, and
  agents have to be told to write it.
- Leave it out (recommended). Most of what a computer lacked is already in the record: commands not
  found, hosts denied, asks and failed installs. What agents learn goes in the repository's
  instruction files, as it does today. It costs the agents' own words on what slowed them down.

Recommended: leave it out. What a computer lacked shows in the record from what happened, which
tells you more than the agent's account of it.
