# How your answer is kept

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. The app asks "Is /work/site/.next a build?" and shows that your
answer will cover every .next folder under /work. You answer yes.

The options:

- A pattern, shown before you answer (recommended). The answer covers /work/site/.next and the .next
  folder of every other project in main, those of last week and those to come. You can narrow it to
  the one folder first. It costs care: a pattern that is too wide marks folders you never looked at.
- The one path asked about. It costs the same question again for each project.

Recommended: a pattern, shown before you answer. Build folders repeat from one project to the next,
and seeing the pattern first keeps the answer to what you meant.

## Notes

"It" is your answer to a question from the app (`questions-to-you`).

That you answered is written in the record as a fact of the kind the part `record` defines for
what you do.
