# Questions to you

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Main holds /work/site/.next, 2.1 GB. Silo cannot tell whether a
rebuild would bring it back, so it counts it as only here. The app asks you: "Is /work/site/.next a
build?" You answer yes, and Disk shows it as coming back by a rebuild, as your opinion.

The options:

- The app asks (recommended). The question waits in the app, and nothing waits for your answer. It
  costs your attention: questions sit beside requests that wait for you, so they have to stay few.
- The app never asks. You correct an opinion where you see it, in Disk or elsewhere. It costs the
  moment: a wrong opinion stays until you happen to look, and new versions of Silo's classifiers
  have few of your answers to be tried on.

Recommended: the app asks. Where Silo cannot tell, you usually can at a glance, and one answer is
enough.
