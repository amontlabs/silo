# What an opinion is

The owner agreed to this in the discussions of 18 and 19 November 2026.

The row of the table "What Silo knows", whole:

| Word | How Silo knows it | Who writes it | Can it be wrong? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opinion** | A classifier, or you, think so. | Classifiers, and you when you give one yourself | Yes. So it says whose it is, it is computed again when its classifier improves, and it is never written into a fact or a report. |
