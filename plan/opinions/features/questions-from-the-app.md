# Questions to you

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. The app asks "Is /work/site/.next a build?" and shows that your
answer will cover every .next folder under /work. You answer yes. Disk then shows those folders as
coming back by a rebuild, as your opinion, and the record notes that you answered.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). Silo asks where it cannot tell, and your answer counts for the past and
  from then on. It costs one more kind of line among what waits for you, so the questions have to
  stay few.
- Leave it out. It costs what you know: a folder you know is a build stays only here, and new
  versions of Silo's classifiers have none of your answers to be tried on.

Recommended: build it. You can tell at a glance what Silo cannot, and one answer covers every folder
like it.
