# A new classifier checked against your answers

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Silo 0.9 brings version 1.3 of the classifier that says how a
path would come back. Before it takes over, it runs on main's record. It agrees with 37 of your 40
answers, where 1.2 agreed with 39, so 1.2 stays and the app tells you.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). It costs keeping two versions side by side, and the run on your record,
  which takes time and, for a classifier that asks a model, tokens.
- Leave it out. New versions take over as they come. It costs a version that does worse on your own
  work going live unnoticed.

Recommended: build it. Your answers are the only test of what right means for your work.
