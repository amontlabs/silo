# Is a budget a rule or a precaution

## The choice

An agent's part, 25 November 2026, from the challenge of #355 (page 6) to `budget-rule-or-precaution`, which
polarzero took, with #351 and #374.

The case: main has a budget of 30 percent of this week's window, and you also use Claude on your
laptop. The boundary sees main's calls exactly, but the provider's figure in each reply is the
window of the whole account. How much of the rise came from main is an estimate, and #355 found it
drifts by a factor of 2 to 4 once the laptop also spends. #374 measured the same: the cap on the
account held at 51 percent, and the share it put on the computer was 0.19 where the computer had
used 0.14.

The options:

- Keep the dollar budgets as rules, add the reserve on the account's window as a rule, and make a
  share per computer a precaution (recommended). The reserve is exact to about 0.3 points (#355).
  It costs a precaution's limits on the share: it can only take protective actions.
- Treat a share of the window as a rule too. It costs the promise that a rule is exact.
- Keep the earlier answer, which says nothing of windows. Then a share budget, which `word-budget`
  allows once taken, has no rule saying how far it can be trusted.

Recommended: the reserve is a rule, a share per computer is a precaution. #351's "at most 30 percent
of this week" is a share, so the rule offered first is the reserve. #355 also proposes a decision
`budget-in-a-window` for how the two are set; it is not needed to take this one.
