# What of each member's spend the team sees

## The choice

An agent's part, 24 November 2026. A lead wants to see on the first of the month what each of 12
developers spent on agents, by provider. Silo runs no server and sends nothing home by default.
The spend is in each developer's record, on each developer's Mac.

The options:

- Daily summaries, written by an extension to a place the team owns (recommended). The summary per
  member and per day holds the dollars spent by provider and model, and each budget reached. It
  leaves out conversation text, commands and file names. The extension reads the spend lines and
  writes to a folder, a bucket or a repository the team picks, through its own rules, as forwarding
  already works (`forwarding-tool`). The member sees what it sends and where. It costs an extension
  each member installs, unless the Mac's management installs it.
- The whole record goes to the team. It costs each person's prompts and conversations going to their
  lead, which few developers accept and which, in the European Union, is monitoring of employees that
  has to be justified and disclosed.
- Nothing leaves. Each member reads their own spend. It costs the lead who pays the bill.

Recommended: daily summaries through an extension. It answers the question a lead asks, how much
and on what, with the least that leaves the Mac, and it adds no mechanism: an extension that reads
the record and sends where its rules allow already exists in the plan.
