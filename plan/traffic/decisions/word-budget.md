# The word "budget"

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Main has a budget of 50 dollars. An agent in it calls a paid
image service with a key you gave it, and spends 30 dollars there.

The options:

- On models (recommended). The budget counts what main's model calls cost, which Silo reads from the
  traffic itself. The 30 dollars of the image service are not counted. It costs you a separate limit
  for any other paid service, set at that service.
- On everything a computer spends, as the word reads now. It costs a promise Silo cannot keep, since
  it sees the price of a model call but not the price of most other paid requests.

Recommended: on models. A budget should say exactly what it counts, and model calls are what Silo
can count exactly.

## Notes

The meaning is worded by whoever wrote the plan, from lines in force. The design record's 4.6 uses the word and gives it no row in its section 2.
