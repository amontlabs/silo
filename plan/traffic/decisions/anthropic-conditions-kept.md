# Does Silo keep Anthropic's two conditions for a product that preinstalls or runs Claude Code

Read on 19 November 2026: Anthropic has a rule for a product that preinstalls or runs Claude Code: the program is not modified, and no sign-in method built into it is removed, disabled or restricted. The rule dates from August 2026, and Anthropic has published no explanation of it.

"Either way": whether a recipe falls under that rule is to find out, `recipe-under-preinstall-rule`.
