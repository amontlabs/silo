# Does any vendor's term bear on changing a reply

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. To deny `git push --force` in Claude Code on your Max plan, Silo
changes a reply Claude Code receives and the later requests of that conversation. The reading of
Anthropic's and OpenAI's terms on 19 November found two clauses to weigh, Anthropic's on reverse
engineering its services and OpenAI's on bypassing protective measures, and left Anthropic's
Consumer Terms and Usage Policy and OpenAI's Sign in with ChatGPT Terms not read in full.

The options:

- Read first, and you decide on any doubt (recommended). Those texts are read in full before denied
  tool calls are built, and each finding goes in this entry's notes, quoted and dated. If a clause
  may forbid the change, denied tool calls are not built for that vendor's program until you decide.
  It costs a wait on denied tool calls, and perhaps the feature itself for one vendor.
- Build on the reading already done. The two clauses found do not seem to cover a denial. It costs a
  risk to your account and to every user's: a clause in a text nobody read in full could forbid the
  change, and a provider could limit the account that relies on it.

Recommended: read first. Denied tool calls are worth building only if they put nobody's account at
risk, and reading three texts in full is little next to that.
