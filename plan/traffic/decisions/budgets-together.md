# When a computer and a conversation in it each have a budget, which one counts

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Main has a budget of 50 dollars and one conversation in it has a
budget of 10. That conversation reaches 10 while main is at 30.

The options:

- Both, first reached stops (recommended). The conversation sends nothing new, and the rest of main
  goes on until 50. It costs a conversation that can stop below its own budget, when main reaches 50
  first.
- The narrower one wins. A conversation with its own budget is no longer bound by the computer's. It
  costs the computer's limit, which that conversation can spend past.
- Only one can be set. Silo refuses a second budget on the same thing, which is simpler. It costs
  the limit for one conversation inside a limited computer.

Recommended: both, first reached stops. Every limit you set should hold, whatever other limit
exists.
