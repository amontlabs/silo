# When you fork a computer that has a budget, what budget does the fork get

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Main has a budget of 50 dollars and an agent makes ten forks of
it.

The options:

- It spends its parent's (recommended). A fork spends from main's 50 dollars unless you give it a
  budget of its own, so ten forks still cannot spend 500. It costs main's agents their budget when
  the forks use it up. One count also has to be shared by several computers.
- Its own fresh copy. Each fork starts with the same budget again. It costs up to 500 dollars for
  the ten forks.
- None until you set one. A fork has no budget until you give it one. It costs the limit itself,
  since forking is then a way around a budget.

Recommended: it spends its parent's. A budget that forking can multiply or escape is not a limit.
