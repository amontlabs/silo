# Nothing waits on a provider's answer

## The choice

An agent's part, 28 November 2026, on polarzero's words of that day: "I don't really like the fact
that we would have to wait", and "anyone can plug their own enterprise proxy and we ship some
enterprise proxy with it".

The case. You run Claude Code on your Claude subscription in main. To show you the model calls,
the boundary ends the encrypted connection to Anthropic, reads the request and sends it on
unchanged. Anthropic's pages describe exactly this for a company's inspecting proxy, sign-in
included (`anthropic-reads-intermediate`). They do not say whether a product that does it for its
users is covered, and the account at risk is yours. With an API key the question does not exist.

The options:

- You choose, and nothing waits (recommended). An API key is always read. For a subscription each
  computer has a setting (`claude-key-or-plan`): read at the boundary, or unread with the program's
  own account as reports. A third setting sends the computer's model calls on to a gateway you
  name. It costs one setting, and a sentence in the app that says what the provider's pages cover
  and what they do not.
- Wait for a written yes before reading any subscription. It costs the first build its model calls
  for everyone who uses a subscription, on an answer that may never come.
- Never read a subscription. It costs refusing a model call at the boundary and knowing that the
  account of the conversation is complete, for the way most people pay.

Recommended: you choose, and nothing waits.

- A Google sign-in stays unread (`google-sign-in-unread`).
