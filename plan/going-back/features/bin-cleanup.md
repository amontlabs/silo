# The bin's cleanup and retention

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You drop a fork. It waits in the bin for its time there, then
goes, and only its record stays. That is already what "Drop and the bin" builds, with the length set
by the decision on the time in the bin.

The options:

- Leave it out (recommended). How long a dropped computer stays, and its removal at the end, are
  built with the drop itself. It costs nothing, as long as emptying the bin by hand goes into "Drop
  and the bin" too.
- Build it as a feature of its own. It costs a second place that says how long the bin keeps a
  computer, and nothing it would add is named.

Recommended: leave it out. Both halves of its name are already in the plan, in the feature that
builds the bin.
