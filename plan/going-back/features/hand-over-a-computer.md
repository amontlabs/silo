# Hand over a computer

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. An agent got a computer into a state where a bug shows, after an
hour of installs and changes. You hand that computer to a colleague, who gets an exact copy on their
own Mac, with its record.

The options:

- Build it, later (recommended). The copy goes without your secrets and sign-ins, as a fork does. It
  costs the size of the copy, tens of GB, and everything else on its disk travels with it, any
  credential kept in a file included. Whether Apple's terms let a macOS computer go to another
  person's Mac is to find out.
- Leave it out. You share the recipe and the repository, and your colleague makes the computer
  again. It costs what is only here, which does not travel, so the bug may not show.

Recommended: build it, later. Reproducing a state for someone else is a common reason to copy a
machine, and with its record the colleague also sees how the computer got there.
