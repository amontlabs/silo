# When you restore a computer, what stays as it is now

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. At 15:00 you take a secret back from main and tighten its rules.
Then you restore main to 14:00.

The options:

- Only the inside (recommended). The disk and the conversations go back. The secret stays taken back
  and the rules stay tight. It costs setting things again by hand if you did want them as they were
  at 14:00.
- Rules and grants too. The computer also gets back the rules and the grants it had then. It costs
  you control, since a restore can reopen without a word something you closed since.

Recommended: only the inside. A restore should never give a computer more than it has now, and the
record of what happened must not be rewound.
