# The rules are the promise

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You set a rule: main reaches GitHub and nothing else. You also
add a classifier that pauses main when a command looks destructive.

The options:

- A principle (recommended). The app tells the two apart: the rule is the promise, and the
  classifier is extra caution. It costs the pitch: Silo never presents a classifier as protection,
  however good it gets.
- Both are protection. The pitch is simpler. It costs a broken promise on the day a classifier is
  fooled.

Recommended: a principle. These are your own words, and it is the split security engineers make
between prevention and detection.

The owner said this: "A classifier can be fooled, and a rule at the boundary cannot be got around.
So the rules are the promise, and a precaution is extra caution on top of them."

The question is worded by whoever wrote the plan. The name is a concept of the design record's
section 12: "Prevention and detection. A rule prevents: it is exact and sits at the boundary. A
classifier detects: it can be fooled and it can be wrong. A bank does the same: a card has limits,
and a fraud score can freeze it. The score never raises a limit. Calling a classifier a boundary
is the mistake to avoid."
