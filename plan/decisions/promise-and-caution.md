# The rules are the promise

The owner said this: "A classifier can be fooled, and a rule at the boundary cannot be got around.
So the rules are the promise, and a precaution is extra caution on top of them."

The question is worded by whoever wrote the plan. The name is a concept of the design record's
section 12: "Prevention and detection. A rule prevents: it is exact and sits at the boundary. A
classifier detects: it can be fooled and it can be wrong. A bank does the same: a card has limits,
and a fraud score can freeze it. The score never raises a limit. Calling a classifier a boundary
is the mistake to avoid."
