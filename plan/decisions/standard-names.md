# Standard names

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. A security engineer reads "A computer cannot get more than it
has" in the Workshop. With "no privilege escalation" beside it, they know at once which idea it is
and what to check Silo against.

The options:

- Carry the names (recommended). Five principles show the field's name: no privilege escalation,
  make illegal states unrepresentable, assume breach, reference monitor, least privilege. It costs
  being held to all that each name means. A reference monitor, for example, is always in the way,
  cannot be tampered with, and is small enough to check.
- Plain sentences only. The principles stay as they read today. It costs an engineer the work of
  matching each one to what they know, and some will take Silo's ideas for home-made ones.

Recommended: carry the names. Each concept gets its standard term, and being held to the full
meaning is a cost worth having.

## Notes

The design record gives none of these five names. They are the names security engineering uses for
the same idea, put forward so that a reader who knows the field recognises each principle.
