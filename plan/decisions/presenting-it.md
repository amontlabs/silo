# Presenting it

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. A developer lands on the home page from a link, and gives it
thirty seconds.

The options:

- The problem first, and what Silo replaces (recommended). The page shows the Mac bought for the
  agent, the spare laptop, the cheap server, and what takes their place. It costs pictures to make,
  a first run that must not fail, and a visitor who cannot file Silo under a known category.
- The category first. The page says "computers for agents". It is filed at once. It costs standing
  next to the products Silo does not want to be compared with.

Recommended: the problem first. People who bought a Mac for their agent know the problem already,
and no category says it for them.

## The first picture, and the video

An agent's part, 24 November 2026.

- What the picture shows depends on the build. With the first build of #348, it is `silo changes`
  after a night: the folders that changed, outside the repository first, and one put back. Once
  receipts exist, it is one receipt: the last run of the whole test suite and its exit code, the
  files changed in and outside the repository, what is still running, and the agent's own last
  sentence beside them.
- Pictures and videos come from a real night with real agents and real models. The morning of #371
  used a scripted model, so its false "All tests pass." was written into the script. It shows the
  shape of a receipt, and cannot be shown as something an agent did.
- Wherever going back is shown, say what it cannot bring back: a push, a deploy, or a request that
  reached another server stays done.
- The short video of going back that the research asks for fits in about 90 seconds once receipts
  exist: the first screen, one receipt, the chain from an alert down to the command, and a put back
  of one folder. With the first build, about 45 seconds: a delete, `silo changes`, a put back.
