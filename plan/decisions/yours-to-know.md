# May Silo hide something that is the user's to know?

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You install an extension that runs code. It gets a computer of
its own, which uses memory and disk on your Mac.

The options:

- A principle (recommended). That computer can be found in the app, with what it uses. It costs one
  more question that every addition has to answer. It also pulls against a short list of what needs
  you, so "shown" has to mean findable, not put in front of you.
- Hide the plumbing. You see less. It costs memory and disk in use with nothing on screen that
  explains it.

Recommended: a principle, with shown meaning findable. People ask for less to look at, and they also
drop a tool the first time it does something behind their back.
