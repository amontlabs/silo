# The word "grant"

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. A fork starts with no secrets. For one task you give it your
GitHub token. Only you, or a rule you set, may do that, so the act needs one name everywhere.

The options:

- Grant (recommended). "To give a computer the ability to do something." It is the word access
  control uses, and it is read as a permission that lasts until it is taken away. It costs a
  slightly formal word on buttons.
- Give. It is the plainest word, and the plan already writes "give it a budget of its own". It is
  read as handing over anything, a file included. It costs the link with "granting", the kind of
  action already in force, and it has no noun for "a grant".

Recommended: grant. It is the standard term, it explains itself, and "granting" is already in force
with the same root. "Allow" is not a candidate: it is what happens to one request.

## Notes

The meaning is worded by whoever wrote the plan, from lines in force. The design record's section 12 gives the concept: "Grant and revoke. The words access control uses for giving and taking back the ability to do something. In Silo only you grant, directly or through a rule you set."
