# The meanings of the words for the boundary

The record's word list (section 2) carries no mark. A word's meaning is in force only where a
line marked Decided or Agreed, or settled in STATE, holds it. What this decision sets is the
word list's own meaning, which replaces the word in `words.json` when taken.

- boundary: in force as "The edge of a computer." (record l.141–144, l.200).
- channel: in force as "One way through the boundary." (record 4.2, l.351–357).
- allow: no marked line holds its meaning.
- hold: no marked line holds its meaning.
- deny: no marked line holds its meaning.
- ask: no marked line holds its meaning.
- secret: no marked line holds its meaning.
