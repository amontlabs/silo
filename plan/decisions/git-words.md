# Git's words

Decided on 18 November 2026.

"One word per idea" belongs to the engineering guide. "Fold, keep and promote are not used" is a
rule for whoever writes the plan: a fork is dropped or becomes main.

The record's word list gives no marked line for what checkpoint, fork and restore mean. Their
meanings are set by `meanings-for-computers`.

Whether "Git for computers" is still the framing is open: `git-for-computers`.
