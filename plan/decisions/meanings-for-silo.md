# The meanings of the words for Silo's pieces

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. The home page says "Silo records what they do there". What
records is neither the app nor the `silo` command: it is a part that runs beside each computer.

The options:

- The whole product (recommended). "The product as a whole: the engine, the app, the `silo` command,
  and the parts of it that run beside and inside each computer." It is read as everything of ours
  that runs. It costs a looser word: where it matters which piece acts, the sentence has to name the
  piece.
- The three pieces you touch. "The product: the engine, the Mac app and the `silo` command." It is
  read as what you install and open. It costs the sentences in force that say Silo for a part beside
  or inside a computer, such as "one thing Silo saw itself", and the list is out of date with the
  first new piece, a phone app for example.

Recommended: the whole product. The plan already says "Silo" for whatever sees, records or refuses,
so the meaning should cover those parts.

## Notes

The record's word list (section 2) carries no mark. A word's meaning is in force only where a
line marked Decided or Agreed, or settled in STATE, holds it. What this decision sets is the
word list's own meaning, which replaces the word in `words.json` when taken.

- Silo: no marked line holds its meaning.
