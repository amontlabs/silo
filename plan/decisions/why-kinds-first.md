# Why the kind comes first

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. A new feature brings a fork back from the bin. Until someone says
that this is granting, nobody knows whether a precaution may do it.

The options:

- Add the reason (recommended). The principle then reads: the kind of every action is named before
  the feature is designed, because the kind of an action decides who may trigger it. It costs
  keeping the reason true if kinds come to decide more than that.
- Leave it without one. The principle stays as it is. It costs a rule that reads as a formality to
  whoever meets it first.

Recommended: add the reason. A rule with its reason is followed where its wording runs out.

The reason is worded by whoever wrote the plan. The design record gives the principle and its
question, not a reason.
