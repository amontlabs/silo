# Is it a channel?

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. A new feature lets an agent paste from your Mac's clipboard into
its computer.

The options:

- A principle (recommended). The clipboard is listed as a channel, has a rule, and is closed until
  you grant it. It costs convenience: each such feature arrives switched off.
- Conveniences on by default. Paste works with no setup. It costs ways through the boundary that
  nobody listed, and with a virtual machine those are what fails, not the machine.

Recommended: a principle. Full access inside is only acceptable because the ways out are few, listed
and yours to open.

Question 1 of the design record's section 12, marked Notes. The sentence is the first of 4.2,
marked Notes. The reason is a concept of section 12, "A boundary is as strong as it is narrow".
