# Git for computers

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. The headline of the home page. "Git for computers" is three words
a developer gets at once: a checkpoint is a commit, a fork is a branch. They then look for merge,
and for nothing else.

The options:

- A comparison, not the headline (recommended). The words stay Git's, and the comparison is used
  where going back is explained. It costs the shortest pitch Silo has.
- Still the framing. The headline says it. It costs a promise of merging, which Silo does not build first, and
  it hides the record, which is the centre of Silo.

Recommended: a comparison, not the headline. A framing should point at the centre of the product,
and this one points at one third of it.

## Notes

The decision `git-words` named it. The words stay Git's, but merging one computer into another
has left the core. At the reset of 18 November 2026 the home page took an interim headline, "Your
agents work in a computer of their own.", and the decision's text was cut down to the words.
