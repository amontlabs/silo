# The word "revoke"

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. At 15:00 you gave a fork your GitHub token. At 16:00 the task is
done and you remove it. The app has to name that act on its button and in the record.

The options:

- Revoke (recommended). "To take back from a computer the ability to do something." It is the word
  access control pairs with grant, and the one GitHub uses for a token. It is read as ending a
  permission. It costs rewriting the sentences of the plan that say "take back" today, in 16 files.
- Take back. It is plainer, and the plan writes it today ("what you granted or took back"). It is
  read as getting something returned, and next to "Silo takes you back to a saved moment" it can be
  read as going back. It costs that confusion, and it has no noun.

Recommended: revoke. A word that can be read as another is replaced, and "take back" sits too close
to going back, which is half of what Silo does.

## Notes

The meaning is worded by whoever wrote the plan, from lines in force. The design record's section 12 gives the concept with "grant": `word-grant`.
