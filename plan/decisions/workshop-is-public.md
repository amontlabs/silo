# Who may use the Workshop

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. A developer finds the Workshop from the home page, signs in with
GitHub and writes that the bin should keep a computer for a month.

The options:

- Anyone signed in can comment, vote and suggest (recommended). You decide, and they are heard. It
  costs noise and spam to clear, and suggestions that you answer or leave unanswered in public.
- Read only. Visitors see the plan, and only you write. It costs every signal from outside on what
  people want.

Recommended: anyone signed in. Building in public is worth little if nobody can answer, and the
verdicts stay yours.
