# Who may use the Workshop

> The Workshop is a public page of silo.amontlabs.com that shows the plan as a map of the
> product's parts. The owner decides there; anyone signed in with GitHub can comment, vote and

The answer leaves out "the decisions are shown to the owner only": in this plan the decisions are
entries, which the Workshop shows to everyone.
