# The words for how far an entry is

The owner's words, on 21 November 2026, about the Workshop:

> It's basically how it works it's transparent about something being not decided, decided, being
> worked on, and built.

The same day the owner was asked "One set of words everywhere: not decided, decided, being built,
built?", with "being built" recorded by the plan, and answered:

> Yes agree with that

An agent's part. Until then the Workshop said "planned" for a feature the owner took, "decided"
for a decision the owner took, and "1 planned", "next" or "done" on a part. Nothing said that a
feature was being built: the plan knew a feature as not decided, taken, or built. A dropped entry
and a replaced decision keep their own words, "dropped" and "replaced".
