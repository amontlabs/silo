# Does it need a new noun?

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Someone proposes "workspaces": a named group of computers that
share settings.

The options:

- A principle (recommended). The proposal is first said with what exists: computers, what Silo
  knows, what Silo does. If it cannot be, it has to argue for the new noun. It costs an awkward
  description now and then, where a new noun would be shorter.
- No principle. A noun is added when a feature wants one. It costs a product that takes longer to
  learn with every feature.

Recommended: a principle. The few nouns are why the plan can be read at all, and the question is
cheap to ask.

Question 2 of the design record's section 12, marked Notes. In full: "Everything is a computer or is
defined through one (a checkpoint is a computer at a moment, a fork is a copy, a recipe is how to
make one, a task is a command in one with an end). Or it is something Silo knows (a fact, a
report, an opinion, with the classifier that gives it), or something Silo does (a rule, a
precaution). A receipt, an alert and the timeline are made from those."
