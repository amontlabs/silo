# May something a promise rests on be best effort?

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You are promised that a held request never waits past its limit.
The limit is kept by a timer. If the timer is "usually on time", so is the promise.

The options:

- A principle (recommended). Everything a promise rests on either works or says that it failed. It
  costs one more question that every addition has to answer. It also costs promises: where only a
  best-effort mechanism exists, Silo calls the thing a caution or does not promise it.
- Case by case. The sentence stays with the limit of a hold, where you said it. It costs the other
  promises, which may rest on something that fails without a word.

Recommended: a principle. A promise is as strong as the weakest thing under it, and a part that
fails silently is how a promise breaks without anyone seeing.
