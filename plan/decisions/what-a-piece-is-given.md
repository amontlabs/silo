# Nothing without a reason

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You install an extension that sums up each evening what main did.
It needs to read main's record. It does not need the network, your secrets or your other computers.

The options:

- A principle (recommended). A computer, an extension or a part of Silo gets only what it gave a
  reason for. The extension reads main's record and nothing else. It costs one more question that
  every addition has to answer. Something that needs more later has to come back and ask you.
- No principle. Each feature chooses what it starts with. It costs defaults that grow one
  convenience at a time, until an extension that sums up a day can also reach the network.

Recommended: a principle. What a thing does not have, it cannot leak or be tricked into using, and
this is the rule security engineers call least privilege.

## Notes

Not in the design record: it was written for the sample of this plan. Its standard name, least
