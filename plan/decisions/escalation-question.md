# No more than it has

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. A new feature lets an agent in a fork ask Silo to copy files into
main.

The options:

- A principle (recommended). The feature is asked whether it gives main or the fork more than it
  has, and who grants that. Here it does, so it asks you. It costs one more question that every
  addition has to answer.
- A decision only. The sentence still binds, as it does today. It costs the question: nothing makes
  a new feature answer it, and two properties that follow from the sentence cannot name where they
  come from.

Recommended: a principle. You decided the sentence already, and this makes every addition answer to
it.

## Notes

The sentence is decided, in `what-an-agent-inside-may-do`. The question is worded by whoever wrote
the plan. The reason is a concept of the design record's section 12: "Access is handed down, never
up. What you give a fork is part of what its parent has, never more."

Once it is taken, `reach-never-more-than-granted` and `fork-never-more-than-parent` follow from it:
their writers named it in `from`, which waits until the principle is in force.
