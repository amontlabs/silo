# Only agents work there

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Your laptop records every file you touch for a day: thousands of
lines, nearly all of them you. Main records the same day: thousands of lines, nearly all of them
your agents.

The options:

- An account of the whole computer, with what was not seen marked (recommended). The reason is said
  as it is, and a missed minute shows as not seen. It costs a weaker line than "complete".
- A complete account. This is the answer as it was written. It is the stronger line. It costs the
  truth on the first minute Silo misses, and you already decided that Silo marks what it did not see
  and never fills it.

Recommended: the whole computer, with what was not seen marked. "Only agents work there" is your
sentence and stays first. "Complete" is a promise nobody knows how to keep.
