# Never fills a hole

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Silo could not watch main for two minutes during a build. The
timeline can show those two minutes as quiet, or as not seen.

The options:

- A principle (recommended). Every addition says what it shows when Silo does not know. The timeline
  shows the two minutes as not seen, so views are less tidy. It costs one more question that every
  addition has to answer.
- A decision only. The sentence still binds, as it does today. It costs the question: a new view can
  show "nothing happened" where the truth is "not seen".

Recommended: a principle. The record is only worth reading if a blank in it always means nothing
happened.

The sentence is in force: `holes`. The question is worded by whoever wrote the plan.

Once it is taken, `books-balance` and `every-loss-is-a-gap` follow from it: their writers named it in
`from`, which waits until the principle is in force.
