# The word "gap"

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. An agent starts 40,000 processes in ten seconds, and 3,000 of the
reports about them are lost on the way to the record. Separately, Silo never writes down the files a
build reads.

The options:

- Only what was lost (recommended). "Gap" names the 3,000 lost reports and nothing else. What Silo
  never records, such as file reads, is not a gap. It costs another way of saying what Silo does not
  look at, wherever that needs saying.
- Everything Silo did not see. "Gap" also names what Silo never records. It costs the word its use,
  since a record with gaps everywhere no longer shows you where something went missing.

Recommended: only what was lost. A gap should tell you that something is missing here that should
have been there.

## Notes

The meaning is worded by whoever wrote the plan, from lines in force. The design record uses the word in lines in force and gives it no row in its section 2.
