# The word for a report the facts agree with

The owner asked for "verified", or at worst "matched".

"Unverified" is the word the owner settled for a report Silo could not check. Its meaning is put
forward by `word-unverified`.

The design record said "corroborated". The owner asked for a plainer word.

On 20 November 2026 the owner agreed to "matched". The verdict waits for the owner's yes to the two
meanings this decision sets.
