# The word for a report the facts agree with

The owner asked for "verified", or at worst "matched".

"Unverified" is said of every report, matched or not (`word-unverified`).

The design record said "corroborated". The owner asked for a plainer word.

On 20 November 2026 the owner agreed to "matched".

The owner, on 21 November 2026: "by essence a report cannot be verified like it can't be 100% sure,
but it can be matched or not, which even then is just an opinion." The two meanings were then
worded as opinions, and the owner read them and agreed: "Yes right, so a report is always by design
unverified, what matters is matched or mismatch."

An agent: a report with nothing to compare it with has no word. It has no such opinion, and it is
simply unverified. "Mismatch" was kept over "unmatched", which could mean either that the facts
disagree or that there was nothing to compare. The owner saw both points with the wording.

An agent: the report "cargo build wrote 48 MB" is matched when the disk grew by 48 MB, and a
mismatch when it grew by 2 MB. How a report is matched to a fact is `matching-reports-to-facts`.
