# When Silo misses something

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. During one run, 3,000 of 40,000 reports about processes are lost.
Later you open the receipt of that run, which lists the commands that ran.

The options:

- Said wherever the run is shown (recommended). The record holds a gap, and the receipt, the
  timeline and the totals of that run say they are incomplete. It costs a mark you will meet on your
  busiest runs.
- Said only in the record. The gap is there for whoever looks for it, and the receipt reads as if it
  were whole. It costs your trust in every receipt once you find one that was not.

Recommended: said wherever the run is shown. Silo never fills a hole, and a hole you are not shown
is one you fill yourself.

## Notes

The record checks the property with a simulator that has a faulty source.
