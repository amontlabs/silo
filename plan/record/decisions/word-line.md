# What one fact or one report in the record is called

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You open main's record at 14:02: a file changed, a connection
opened, and an agent said it ran the tests. Each of the three is one line.

The options:

- One fact or one report, as it is written in the record (recommended). The word says what a line
  holds, whatever it is about. It costs nothing more to learn, since a line is what you see in the
  record.
- One row of the record, the meaning as it was written. It is read as a row of a table. It costs a
  meaning that says how a line looks and nothing of what it holds.

Recommended: one fact or one report. A word should say what a thing is, not how it looks.

Split on 22 November 2026 from `sources-number-their-lines`, which keeps how Silo knows that lines
are missing.
