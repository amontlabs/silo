# The word "unverified"

The meaning is worded by whoever wrote the plan, from lines in force. polarzero settled "unverified" in place of "as told", for a report.

An agent, on 21 November 2026: the meaning first put forward was "Said of a report Silo could not
check." It read as one of three states, beside matched and mismatch, as if a matched report were
verified.

polarzero, the same day: "by essence a report cannot be verified like it can't be 100% sure, but it
can be matched or not, which even then is just an opinion." And, on reading the present wording:
"Yes right, so a report is always by design unverified, what matters is matched or mismatch."

Whether the facts agree with a report is `report-matched` in the part `record`.
