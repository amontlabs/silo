# Where is the record's own disk use shown

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Your Mac has 40 GB left. Main takes 120 GB, its checkpoints 60
GB, and the record 12 GB, of which 9 GB are recordings.

The options:

- In Disk, with everything else (recommended). The record is one entry beside main and its
  checkpoints, with its limit. It costs Disk showing something that is in no computer.
- In the settings, beside its limit. Disk shows computers only. It costs a second place to look when
  disk is short.

Recommended: in Disk. When disk is short you want everything Silo takes in one place.
