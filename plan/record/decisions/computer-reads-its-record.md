# What an agent inside a computer may do and read

The second half of the question, what a computer may see of the opinions and precautions about
it, is settled in the part `rules`.

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. An agent in main uses Silo's command to list everything the app
shows for main. The list holds the check that pauses main when its work looks unsafe, and what that
check concluded. The plan calls that check a precaution and what it concluded an opinion.

The options:

- Everything except the checks (recommended). An agent inside reads and does what you could for its
  computer, except see the precautions set on it and the opinions about it. It costs its answers
  about them, since it cannot explain a pause to you.
- Everything you can see. An agent inside reads all the app shows for its computer, precautions
  included. It costs the precautions their strength, since the agent can learn what triggers them
  and work around it.

Recommended: everything except the checks. It is polarzero's own reason in `where-precautions-live`: the
agent should not know what can trigger a precaution.

Two decisions polarzero took say that an agent reads everything, `what-an-agent-inside-may-do` and
`silo-command-reads-the-app`, so this one replaces both and keeps the rest of what they say. The
lines of the record about an opinion or a precaution are hidden with them. The audit's finding is in
the notes of `what-an-agent-inside-may-do`.
