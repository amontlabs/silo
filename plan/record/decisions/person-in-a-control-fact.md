# Which person a control fact names

## The choice

An agent's part, 24 November 2026. Two people of a team reach the computers on one host. One of
them takes back a secret at 14:02. Every control fact says who did it: "you, a rule, a precaution,
an extension or Silo" (`who-made-each-change`). On that host "you" is two people.

The options:

- A person's key, from the first version of the record (recommended). Each person reaches the
  engine with a key of their own, as with SSH. A control fact made by a person names that key's
  person. With one person on a Mac it is the same name every time. It costs one field per control
  fact.
- Added when a host is shared. It costs a change to the record's format, and records written
  before it that cannot say who.

Recommended: a person's key from the first version. It is cheap now and a format change later.
