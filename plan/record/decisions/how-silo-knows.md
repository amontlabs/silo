# How Silo knows a thing

The record's table, without its row for an opinion, which is in the part `opinions`. The words
`fact` and `report` are in `words.json`.

| Word | How Silo knows it | Who writes it | Can it be wrong? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fact | Silo saw it itself, from outside the computer. | The engine and the parts of Silo at the boundary | No. What was not seen is recorded as a gap. |
| Report | A program inside the computer told Silo. | Silo's probe, a small part of Silo that runs inside the computer, and an agent program's own account of itself | It is what the inside said. An agent with full access can interfere with it. |
