# What does Silo do when the record's size limit is still reached once bulky content has gone

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You set the record's limit at 20 GB. No recording and no message
text is left to remove, the lines alone take 20 GB, and three agents are working in main.

The options:

- Silo asks you (recommended). It warns you well before. At the limit it asks you to raise the limit
  or to remove the record of computers that are gone. Until you answer, agents carry on with what
  was already allowed, and anything that needs a new decision waits. It costs agents blocked while
  you are away.
- The oldest lines go. Recording carries on and nobody waits. It costs the trail, since an agent can
  push out what it did by making noise.
- Keep writing past the limit. Nobody waits and nothing is removed. It costs your disk, since the
  limit is then not a limit.

Recommended: Silo asks you. Lines are small, so this is rare, and it is the one answer that neither
loses the trail nor ignores the limit you set.
