# What the record may cost in speed

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. A build takes 10 minutes in a computer with nothing recorded. In
the trials, one way of seeing file changes as they happen made a build 1.6 to 2.1 times slower.

The options:

- 5 percent (recommended). The build takes at most 10 minutes 30 seconds. It costs the live ways of
  watching files that were measured, which do not fit, so file changes are then learned at each
  checkpoint.
- Up to twice as slow. Every file change is seen as it happens. It costs a 10 minute build that
  takes up to 20.
- No number until it is measured. The promise stays as it is written today. It costs a promise with
  nothing in it, and nothing to refuse a slow design against.

Recommended: 5 percent, as a target to confirm by measuring. Past a few percent, people notice and
turn recording off.
