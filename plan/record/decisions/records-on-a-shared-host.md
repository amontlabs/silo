# Records on a shared host

## The choice

An agent's part, 25 November 2026. Ana's main on the team's host has run an agent all night. Its
record holds the model conversations, the commands and their output, the files changed. Ben wants
to see why the staging database changed at 03:12. Ana's record also holds a conversation where she
pasted a customer's email.

The options:

- Its owner, and whom they share it with (recommended). A main the team shares is read by everyone
  who follows it (`teams`). The record is kept in its owner's folder as their Unix user
  (`members-on-a-host`), so another person's process cannot open it. The host's administrator can,
  and Silo says so. It costs Ben asking Ana.
- Everyone on the host reads every record. It costs what Ana's record holds that is not Ben's to
  read.
- Each person's own only, no sharing. It costs the incident case above.

Recommended: its owner and whom they share it with. A shared receipt (#362, `shared-receipt-signed`)
covers what a reviewer needs without the whole record.
