# The record of a computer

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. An agent worked in main all night. In the morning you ask what it
reached, what it spent and what it changed, and you want an answer the agent could not have
rewritten.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). Silo writes down, outside main, what it saw and what programs inside told
  it. Each line is written once, with its time and where it came from. It costs disk on your Mac and
  some speed in main, each with a limit.
- Leave it out. Silo runs computers and takes checkpoints, with no account of what happened in them.
  It costs nearly everything else: fifteen features read the record, receipts and rules among them.

Recommended: build it. The record is the centre of Silo.
