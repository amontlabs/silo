# Gaps in the record

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. During one run, 3,000 of 40,000 reports about processes are lost.
You open the receipt of that run.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). The record holds a gap for the 3,000, and the receipt says the run is
  incomplete. It costs a mark on your busiest runs.
- Leave it out. The receipt lists what arrived and looks whole. It costs every receipt its worth,
  since you cannot tell a complete one from one that lost a tenth.

Recommended: build it. Silo never fills a hole, and this is what shows the hole.
