# Does main copy from the Mac, or start from a guided recipe

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Your Mac has Claude Code, Codex, forty tools installed with
Homebrew and an app you installed by hand. You make main.

The options:

- A recipe written from your Mac (recommended). The first run lists what it found, you tick what to
  keep, and main is made from that list. Your settings and repositories are copied in once. It costs
  what a recipe cannot describe: the app installed by hand is listed and left out, and you install
  it in main yourself. How much of a usual Mac a recipe can describe is still to find out.
- A copy of your Mac's user. Main gets your whole user account, as Migration Assistant would move it
  to a new Mac. It costs what comes with it: every login, key and file you forgot you had, in a
  computer where agents have full access, and a main that cannot be made again.
- A guided recipe from scratch. Silo asks which programs and tools you want and ignores your Mac. It
  costs setting up your habits again by hand.

Recommended: a recipe written from your Mac. You keep your setup, main can be made again if it
breaks, and nothing comes in that you did not see.
