# The manifest is enforced

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You install Disk watch 1.2 on main. It may read /work on main and
alert you, and it wakes once a minute. Its program then tries to read what main's agents sent to
their model, and to wake on every new line of main's record. Both are refused, and the record says
so. A month later its author publishes 1.3, which also asks to read what main's agents sent to their
model.

The options:

- Held to what you accepted (recommended). Disk watch can do only what you saw when you installed
  it, on the computers you chose. Version 1.3 changes nothing until you import and install it, and
  1.2 keeps running as you accepted it until then. It costs a new look at the list each time an
  extension asks for more.
- Updates switch on by themselves. A new version replaces the old one with whatever it asks for, as
  editor extensions often do. It costs your say: one update of an extension you trusted for one job
  can read your conversations and send them to any host its computer may reach.

Recommended: held to what you accepted. The list you read before installing is only worth reading if
it stays true, which is why Chrome turns off an extension whose update asks for more until you
agree.

## Notes

The standard model: what is accepted at install is what is enforced.
