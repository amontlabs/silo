# The finding of an extension

## The choice

An agent's part, 27 November 2026. The transaction guard holds a transaction because its
simulation shows 2 ETH leaving your account for an address you never used. `what-an-ask-shows`
writes an ask from facts: the computer, the host, the secret. The one thing you need in order to
answer, what the simulation found, has no place in it. An opinion takes its value from a list Silo
fixes (`who-fixes-the-values`), and a balance is not in any list.

The options:

- A finding, shown as the extension's words (recommended). The value of the opinion stays in
  Silo's list, and the finding is the explanation `opinion-is-a-value` already allows. The ask
  keeps Silo's facts and the extension's words apart, as it does for what the agent says. It costs
  text from someone else's code in front of you at the moment you decide.
- Values only. The ask says "held by transaction-guard". It costs the reason, and you open a panel
  to find it while the hold runs out.
- The extension writes the ask. It costs the facts: an ask could then say anything.

Recommended: a finding, shown as the extension's words. No rule and no precaution reads a
finding. A threshold, such as how much may leave, lives in the extension.
