# How Silo is extended

The record gives this sentence as what the notes already say: "The notes already say it: Silo is
extended by a client of the engine's calls or by a program inside a computer, and nothing loads
into the engine or the app at run time." (record 5.5, l.849–852). Its mark is Notes.

The record's sentence said Silo is extended "by a client of the engine's calls". That does not
hold with the word `extension`, which is rules, a computer, or both, and with
`extension-ships-a-computer`: whatever an extension executes runs in a computer made from its
recipe. A program on the Mac that calls the engine is a client, and executes outside any computer.
