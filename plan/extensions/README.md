# What you can add

Extensions: what someone else made, or you, and you add to Silo.

## For

To let people watch a lot of agent work in their own way, without giving what they add any power
to open or delete something.

## Why it is so

- **What is executed runs in a computer of its own** (`back-end-runs-in-a-computer`). What Silo
  only reads, rules and panels, runs nowhere.
- **It reaches Silo only through the tools it declared**, and installing it grants them
  (`extension-tools`, `import-then-install`).
- **No tool grants or destroys** (`what-an-extension-never-does`). A tool reads, shows or
  protects, and one that protects sets a precaution (`extension-may-protect`).

These are put forward and not in force yet. In force today: `added-classifier-cannot-act`,
`where-a-classifier-runs-is-shown`.

## Three examples

- **Registry rules.** No back end. It adds rules that allow `crates.io` on a computer. Importing
  it shows those rules, and installing it switches them on.
- **Disk watch.** Its back end wakes every minute, reads `/work` on main, shows a panel and
  notifies you. It can stop nothing.
- **Request check.** Its back end is asked when a network request of main waits at the boundary.
  It answers: let it go on, deny it, or hold it. It can never allow one that the rules deny.
