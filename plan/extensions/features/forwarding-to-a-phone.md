# Forwarding to a phone

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You are out. A rule holds a push from main for your yes. Your
phone buzzes: "main: push of 3 commits to amontlabs/site waits for you". You get back to your Mac
and answer it in Silo before the hold runs out.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). Alerts and asks reach you wherever you are, long before Silo has a phone
  app. It costs the text of each alert and ask, which leaves your Mac for the phone service, and a
  small computer that runs for the forwarder. You still answer in Silo.
- Leave it out. You learn of an ask when you look at your Mac. It costs the asks that run out while
  you are away, and the work that waits behind them.

Recommended: build it. Agents work while you are away, and an ask nobody sees ends as a refusal.
