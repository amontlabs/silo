# Taking an extension back

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Disk watch is installed on main and on two forks, and it alerts
you too often. You take back its tools on the forks, then remove it from main. Its rules are
switched off, its computer goes to the bin like any dropped computer, and its opinions go. The
record keeps what it did.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). Whatever you install, you can take back, one tool at a time or all at
  once. It costs one thing at the moment you do it: a request that is waiting for its answer is
  denied or left to your rules, as you chose when installing it.
- Leave it out. An extension, once installed, stays with every tool you granted it. It costs your
  way back from a choice you regret.

Recommended: build it, at the same time as installing. An extension you cannot remove is one you
should not install.
