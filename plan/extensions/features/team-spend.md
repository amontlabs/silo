# Spend across a team

## The choice

An agent's part, 24 November 2026. A lead pays for 12 developers' agents, gives each $1,500 a
month, and finds one at $2,200. Vendor caps cover one product each, and teams say they are easy to
get around.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). Each member's Silo holds a budget on all of that member's computers
  (`team-budget`), and an extension writes a daily summary of each member's spend to a place the
  team owns (`spend-the-team-sees`). It costs a pooled budget across Macs, which is not offered.
- Leave it out. It costs the pain teams already have a budget for.

Recommended: build it. Of everything a team asks of Silo, this is the one a lead already pays to
solve.
