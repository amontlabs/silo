# No approval by hand

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You make a macOS computer from a recipe that includes computer
use. An agent opens your Mac app and clicks through its sign-up, and no dialog ever asked you to
allow screen recording or control of the computer.

The options:

- No approval by hand (recommended). Silo sets the approvals while it makes the computer, and its
  forks keep them. It costs a way Apple does not offer: the approvals are written with a macOS
  protection turned off inside the computer, and a macOS update can break it. Whether it works on
  the current macOS is still to find out. When it fails, Silo says which approval is missing, and
  you give it once in that computer.
- Approve once by hand. After a computer is made you click through the dialogs once, and its forks
  keep them. It costs a minute per new computer, and a fresh computer made for a task cannot use
  computer use, since nobody is there to click.

Recommended: no approval by hand. Fresh computers made for tasks have nobody to click, and a recipe
should make a computer ready to work.

## Notes

The owner said this on 18 November 2026. "Approval" here is the operating system's permission,
not an ask of Silo's. Whether it can be done is `computer-use-approvals`.
