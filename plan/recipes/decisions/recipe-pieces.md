# Recipes made of pieces

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You want a computer with Claude Code, Node 22 and computer use.
Its recipe names macOS and three pieces: claude-code, node-22 and computer-use. None of the three
makes a computer by itself.

The options:

- A piece adds one thing (recommended). A recipe names a system and the pieces it includes. A piece
  adds one thing to a computer, and it can include other pieces. It costs a second word to learn
  beside "recipe".
- A piece is a small recipe. Any recipe can include any other. It costs clarity. Two recipes that
  each name a system can be combined, and which system wins has to be settled each time. "Piece"
  then names nothing that a recipe is not.

Recommended: a piece adds one thing. Your two examples, the tools of the computer that watches the
others and computer use, each add one thing and make no computer.

## Notes

"A recipe is made of pieces that combine" is the owner's, 18 November 2026. The second sentence
is a proposal, and sets the word `piece`.

The owner's two examples of pieces: the tools of the computer that watches the others, and
computer use.
