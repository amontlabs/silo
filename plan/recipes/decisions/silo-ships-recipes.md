# Who writes recipes

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You install Silo and make main from Silo's recipe for macOS with
Claude Code, adding Postgres to it. A colleague sends you her recipe for a Rust computer, and you
make a computer from it.

The options:

- Silo's and your own (recommended). You start from a recipe that works, and you add what Silo lacks
  yourself. It costs Silo the upkeep of its recipes. A recipe someone sends you can name hosts and
  secrets, and those still wait for your yes.
- Only your own. Silo ships no recipe. It costs your first hour with Silo, spent writing a recipe
  before any agent can work.

Recommended: Silo's and your own. Making a computer must be convenient, which needs a working start,
and your own tools need recipes of your own.

## Notes

The owner said this on 18 November 2026. A place where people share recipes is the later feature
`sharing-place`, in `extensions`.
