# The meanings of the words for the boundary

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. The word list gives "channel" a meaning that holds the list of
the seven channels, and "deny" a meaning that holds the rule that everything is denied unless
allowed. The day a feature adds an eighth channel, such as opening a link in the browser on your
Mac, the word itself has to be decided again.

The options:

- One sentence that says what the thing is (recommended). Allow, hold, deny, ask and secret each get
  a short meaning. Boundary and channel keep the meanings polarzero already took. The list of channels and
  the rule on what is denied stay in their own decisions. It costs a reader of the word list one
  more place to look for the list of channels.
- The meanings as the old word list has them. Each word also carries what follows from it. It costs
  deciding a word again each time a list or a rule it carries changes, and the same thing is then
  said in two places that can drift apart.

Recommended: one sentence that says what the thing is. A word is used everywhere in Silo, so its
meaning should be the part that changes least.

word list's own meaning, which replaces the word in `words.json` when taken.

- boundary: in force as "The edge of a computer." (record l.141–144, l.200); "wall" has no marked source, so it is in this decision's `not` and not in force.
