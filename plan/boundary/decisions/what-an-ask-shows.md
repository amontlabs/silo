# What does an ask show you

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. An ask says that main wants to send 2 MB to api.example.com with
your deploy key. The agent adds a line: "I need this to publish the fix you asked for."

The options:

- Silo's words, and the agent's reason marked as its own (recommended). Silo says what would pass,
  from what it saw. The agent's line is shown under it as the agent's words. It costs you a line
  that a hostile agent can write to talk you into a yes.
- Silo's words only. The ask shows what would pass and nothing from the agent. It costs you the
  reason: you see a push or an upload and have to open the conversation to learn why.

Recommended: Silo's words, and the agent's reason marked as its own. Silo already tells apart what
it saw from what it was told, and an ask with no reason gets a yes without thought or a no without
cause.
