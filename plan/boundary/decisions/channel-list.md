# Which channels does a computer have

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. A program in a fork has to sign you in to a service, so it needs
a link opened in the browser on your Mac. The list of channels has seven entries and that is not one
of them.

The options:

- A list that grows with the features (recommended). Seven channels today. Each says what it lets
  through, in which direction and what is at the other end. A feature that makes something new cross
  the edge of a computer adds its channel, and you see it in the list. It costs a longer list to
  read as Silo grows.
- Seven channels, fixed. The list is the one written today. It costs every later feature that fits
  none of them: it is either not built, or it crosses the edge of a computer without showing in the
  list.

Recommended: a list that grows with the features. What matters is that nothing crosses without being
in the list, not that the list stays short.

## Notes

- The first sentence comes from the record's section 2, on the word "channel". Section 4.2 gives the
  same list, with repository access and without the way in from your Mac. That change is put to the
  owner in `reaching-a-computer`. The second sentence is 4.2, marked Notes.
- That every channel is listed, has a rule, and none exists by default is the principle put
  forward by the root decision `channel-question`.
