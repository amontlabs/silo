# What part may a classifier play in an ask

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. An ask says that main wants to push to github.com/you/app. Under
it, a line says that the classifier you installed finds the push low risk.

The options:

- Shown as an opinion (recommended). The ask shows what the classifier thinks, marked as its
  opinion, and you answer. It costs some of your attention: a reassuring line from a classifier that
  was fooled makes a wrong yes more likely.
- Only warnings are shown. The ask shows an opinion only when it says something looks wrong. It
  costs the help on the asks you cannot assess yourself, where "looks fine" would have saved you the
  reading.
- No opinion on an ask. You answer from what the request is, and nothing else. It costs you the
  warning a classifier would have given.

Recommended: shown as an opinion. You asked for classifiers to help you read less, and the ask says
whose opinion it is and that it can be wrong. The yes stays yours in every case.

## Notes

What an opinion may lead to is said by `opinion-acts-through-a-precaution` in `rules`.
