# The word "request"

The meaning is worded by whoever wrote the plan, from lines in force. The owner settled "request" in place of "the thing", for what waits at the boundary. Its other uses in the design record are written "what you asked the agent" and "an effect asks a driver".

An agent, on 21 November 2026: the meaning first put forward was "What waits at the boundary."
Not every request waits. `store-messages-once` records which messages each request sent, and that
request to a model may pass without waiting. `allow-hold-deny-only-synchronous` says "one request
that is still waiting". The owner read the present wording and agreed to it the same day.
