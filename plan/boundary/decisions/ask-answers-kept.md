# Your answers to asks are kept

## The choice

An agent's part, 27 November 2026. Over a month you answer two hundred asks. Each answer says
whether one held request should have passed. That is the only measure there is of whether an
extension that holds requests is right, and of whether a new one would do better
(`scoring-a-classifier`, `extension-tried-live`). `how-your-answer-is-kept` keeps your answers to
questions about paths. No entry keeps an answer to an ask.

The options:

- Kept as your opinion about that request (recommended). It costs nothing you do not already do.
  The answers lean one way: you only answer what something held, so they say little about what
  passed.
- Not kept. The ask ends and only the fact that the request passed or failed stays. It costs the
  measure.

Recommended: kept as your opinion about that request.
