# Which words name the boundary and what waits there

In force. The design record's section 2, with polarzero's later words: "request" in place of "the thing". The record gives allow, hold and deny in one row, and synchronous and asynchronous in another; each is a word of its own here. What "request" means is still to decide: `word-request`.

The word list itself carries no mark. Meanings that no marked line holds are set by
`meanings-for-the-boundary`.
