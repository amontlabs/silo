# The ways out of a computer

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Before you give main a token, you open main's page. It lists the
network, limited to github.com and crates.io, port 3000 open to your Mac, no files, the clipboard
off, and your terminal as the way in.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). Each line is one way data can leave or enter, with its direction. It costs
  upkeep on a hard promise: each new channel has to be added to the list, and one missed channel
  makes the page say something untrue. Tests that attack a real computer have to cover it.
- Leave it out. You work out what a computer can reach from its rules and settings, one screen at a
  time. It costs you the answer to "where can this go?" at the moment you grant something.

Recommended: build it. An allowed host is still a way out, so the list of ways out is what you read
before every grant.
