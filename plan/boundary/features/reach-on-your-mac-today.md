# What agents on your Mac can reach today

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Before installing Silo, you run a command on your Mac. It lists
what an agent started in your terminal can read today: 14 SSH keys, the tokens in your shell's
configuration, your cloud credentials, the `.env` files of 30 repositories.

The options:

- Build it. A newcomer sees a concrete reason for a computer of the agents' own. It costs a second
  small product to build and keep right, a scan of your Mac that Silo does not otherwise need. It
  also sells by fear, and this audience asks for full access without worry, not for safety.
- Leave it out (recommended). The home page says what agents get with Silo. It costs the one
  demonstration that needs no install of anything.

Recommended: leave it out. It is in the stage "later" with nothing depending on it, and what it
shows can be said in two sentences on the site.
