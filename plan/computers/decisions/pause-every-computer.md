# Can you pause every running computer at once

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. At 23:00 you see agents in main and two forks pushing to the
wrong branch, and you want everything frozen now. An hour earlier a precaution had paused a third
fork because a classifier said "not safe".

The options:

- Pause all, and resume only what it paused (recommended). One click freezes main and the two forks.
  One click later brings back those three, and the third fork stays paused until you look at why. It
  costs a second step for each computer paused for another reason.
- Pause all, resume all. One click brings back every computer. It costs the precaution's pause: the
  third fork resumes without anyone looking at what the classifier saw.

Recommended: resume only what it paused. Pausing everything is for an emergency, and undoing it
should not undo anything else.
