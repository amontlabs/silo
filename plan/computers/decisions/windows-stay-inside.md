# Can a window an agent opens appear on your device

## For the owner

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You type in a document on your Mac while an agent in main tests
the Mac app it builds. The app opens three windows and a dialog.

The options:

- Windows stay inside (recommended). Nothing appears on your Mac and nothing takes your keyboard.
  You see main's windows when you open main's screen in Silo. It costs a step to look at an app an
  agent opened, and you cannot keep one of main's apps as a window beside your own.
- Windows on your Mac, as Parallels shows a virtual machine's apps among yours. It costs your focus,
  since agents' windows open while you type. It also costs trust in what you see: a hostile agent
  can draw a window that looks like your Mac asking for your password.

Recommended: windows stay inside. Agents open windows all day, and a window on your Mac that you did
not open should never be theirs.
