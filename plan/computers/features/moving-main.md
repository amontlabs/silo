# Moving main

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. You get a new Mac. Main, with its repositories, its tools, its
checkpoints and its record, moves to it, and its agents' conversations go on there.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). Main moves whole to the other machine. It costs copying tens of GB, and
  limits: a macOS main moves only to another Mac, and secrets Silo holds outside main may have to be
  entered again.
- Leave it out. On a new machine you make main again from its recipe. It costs what is not in a
  recipe: installed tools, caches, conversations and the record.

Recommended: build it, in its later stage. Main is meant to last longer than one Mac.
