# Main on several devices

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. Main runs on your Mac at the office. At home, on your laptop, you
open Silo, see main's agents, its record and its screen, and pause a fork.

The options:

- Build it (recommended). One main, reached from each of your devices, while it runs on one of them.
  It costs a way into main from outside the machine it runs on, which has to be guarded, and a main
  on a Mac that sleeps or goes offline cannot be reached.
- Leave it out. You reach main only from the machine it runs on. It costs your work away from that
  machine, unless main runs on a server.

Recommended: build it, in its later stage. One main reached from anywhere fits how people already
use a remote machine.

## A Mac that stays on, 24 November 2026

An agent's part, from research on who would use Silo first. It adds a case and keeps the
recommendation.

People who run agents unattended buy a Mac mini for them and leave it on: Mac minis sold out in
spring 2026, and the press tied it to this. A laptop cannot do that job. With its lid closed, a
MacBook sleeps unless it is on power with an external display attached, and main stops with it.
So for the people who run agents overnight, main is likely to run on a Mac mini with no display,
and they reach it from the laptop they carry. That is this feature's case, and it argues for
building it earlier than its place in "Later". Two questions follow from it, still to decide:

- Whether the Mac that runs main can be one nobody sits at: Silo started at login with no window,
  and its first run done from the other device.
- Whether "main on several devices" needs main reached from outside the local network, or whether
  most such Mac minis sit on the same network as the laptop at night.
