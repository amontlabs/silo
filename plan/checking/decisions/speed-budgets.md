# Which operations have a speed budget

"Budget" here is the record's word for a target of speed. The word `budget`, a limit on spending, is not
in force: it is put forward by `word-budget`.

In the record, "The overhead of keeping the record is measured the same way" comes straight after
the line on budgets (9.8, l.1781–1786). The cost of the record itself is the property
`record-overhead-is-bounded`, in the part `record`.
