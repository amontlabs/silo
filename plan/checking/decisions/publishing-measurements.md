# When measurements are published

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. For Silo 0.4, forking main took 180 ms at the 99th percentile and
410 ms at worst, over 500 interleaved runs on a Mac mini M4 with macOS 26.1, on 3 December 2026. The
memory of an idle computer was 1.4 GB, against a budget of 1 GB.

The options:

- Every budget, met or missed (recommended). Both numbers are published, the second marked as
  missed. It costs a public page that shows where Silo is slow, which others can quote.
- Only once they hold. The fork time is published, and the memory waits until it meets its budget.
  It costs trust: readers cannot tell a number not measured yet from one that looks bad, and a page
  of only good numbers reads as marketing.

Recommended: every budget, met or missed. You asked to measure excellently and make a difference
there, and people trust measurements that show their misses.
