# What is a property's sentence for

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. A promise reads: "A restore changes only what is inside the
computer. The record, what was spent, the rules and precautions, and what was granted or taken back
stay as they are." The site shows that sentence. Silo's tests replay a million made-up histories
against it, and a test that attacks a real computer tries to make a restore bring back a secret you
took back.

The options:

- One sentence, word for word (recommended). It costs promises that read like plain specifications
  and not like slogans. Changing one goes through you and through its checks.
- The site words it its own way, such as "Restores never touch your settings". It costs a promise
  that says more or less than what is checked: this one leaves out the record and says "settings",
  which nothing checks.

Recommended: one sentence, word for word. What you are told is then exactly what was tested.
