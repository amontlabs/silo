# Which properties does Silo promise in public

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. The page of promises for Silo 0.4 has 14 candidates. "A restore
changes only what is inside the computer" passes its checks. The promise that every way out of a
computer is shown has no test on a real computer yet.

The options:

- Only what passes (recommended). The first is promised. The second is not, until its check passes,
  and the Workshop still shows it as planned. A promise whose check fails at a later release comes
  off the page until it passes again. It costs a short page at first, and promises in the plan that
  wait for their checks.
- Every promise in the plan. Both are on the page. It costs a promise nothing has tested, which is
  the kind that turns out false.

Recommended: only what passes. A promise is worth what its check is worth.
