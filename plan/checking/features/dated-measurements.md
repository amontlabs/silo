# Published measurements

## The choice

An agent's part, 22 November 2026. The site shows: "Fork of main: 180 ms at the 99th percentile, 410
ms at worst, over 500 interleaved runs. Mac mini M4, macOS 26.1. Silo 0.4, 3 December 2026." Beside
it: "Memory of an idle computer: 1.4 GB, target 1 GB, missed."

The options:

- Build it (recommended). It costs a run of every measurement at each release, and a public page
  that shows where Silo is slow.
- Leave it out. It costs every speed claim Silo makes, since a number with no date and no machine is
  not one people can check.

Recommended: build it. Speed is a requirement of Silo, and published numbers are how people hold it
to that.
