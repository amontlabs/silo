Add macOS computers on Apple Silicon Macs. Choose macOS in New computer: Silo downloads the newest macOS your Mac can run from Apple, installs it, and sets it up for computer use with no clicks (an account, SSH, System Integrity Protection turned off, and LCU with its Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions). macOS computers sit in the Computers list with a macOS badge; you can start, stop and delete them, open their screen in a native window, and move text and images with Paste into computer and Copy from computer. Checkpoints, remote computers and the Linux computer tools are not available for macOS computers yet.
