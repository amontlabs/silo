A finished request no longer shows "Running…" in Pending when Silo no longer keeps its result. The "Quitting Silo" screen that appears when Silo's engine is being stopped by something else (the command line, an update or another device) now goes away as soon as the engine disconnects or reconnects, and it gives way to the Quit question so Cancel leaves Silo usable.
