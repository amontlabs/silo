######## notes
- An earlier pass of the same script on the first fixed build (before a one-line refactor that removed an extra
  `inspect` call) also passed 14/14 at both kill points; its 5 s kill caught e2e-r7-c "starting" and its 6 s kill
  caught it "running", the two states that failed before the fix. In the final pass above the 5 s kill happened
  to catch it "stopped" and the 6 s kill "running".
- T15 crash 8 (kill 2 s into the swap): the failure was not an exec hang. Cause found with
  checks/r10_crash8_probe.sh (kill at journal step "swapped" + 0.5 s, 2 of 2 failures before the fix, 0 of 5 after):
  the rebuilt computer's first start makes a "Before network policy change" checkpoint; the engine was killed
  right after the runtime finished it, so the next `silo start` refused with "An interrupted checkpoint could not be
  removed. Retry after its dependencies are released." and `silo exec` answered "Start <name> first" until the
  script's 180 s wait ran out. The T15 run after the fix is evidence/T15-after-R10.txt (173 pass, 0 fail, 3 skipped).
