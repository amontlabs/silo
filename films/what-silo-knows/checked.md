# Checks of what-silo-knows

Each time this film was behind the plan and was found to still hold: the date, the entries of the plan it
was behind on, and why what the film says is still right. Written by `node tools/behind.mjs what-silo-knows --holds`.

## 2026-11-21

Behind on: `word-unverified`.

The film says of a report "Written once, unverified." and of a result mixed with a report "the result is unverified: no surer than the report", which is the new meaning: every report is unverified, and so is a result computed from reports. It never speaks of matched or mismatch, so it does not set them beside unverified as states.
